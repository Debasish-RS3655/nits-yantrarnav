Publish origin coordinates: Phase is set after publishing origin coordinates
ros2 topic pub position/origin std_msgs/String "data: 'x=0.0 y=0.0 z=0.0'" --once

Publish yellow boundary detected:
ros2 topic pub /yellow_boundary_found std_msgs/Bool "data: true" --once

Publish edge coordinates: 
ros2 topic pub /edge_coordinates std_msgs/String "data: 'x=0 y=0 z=1, x=4 y=0 z=1, x=4 y=3 z=1, x=0 y=3 z=1'" --once

Publish detected flat areas:
ros2 topic pub /position/flat_area std_msgs/String "data: 'x=1.50 y=2.00 z=0.50, x=3.00 y=1.00 z=0.50, x=2.00 y=3.50 z=0.50'" --once

Publish landed:
ros2 topic pub /launch_land_status std_msgs/String "data: 'landed'" --once

Publish launched:
ros2 topic pub /launch_land_status std_msgs/String "data: 'launched'" --once

Listen to drone commands topic:
ros2 topic echo /drone_commands
